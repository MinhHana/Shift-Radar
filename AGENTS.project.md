# Shift Radar — product north star

The user is a software/ML engineer. They do **not** have time to scroll X or GitHub.

This app is their right hand: **the full AI briefing**, not a niche filter.

## What to surface

1. **Everything material** about AI on X and GitHub: trends, tools, CLIs, models, papers, agents, APIs, open and closed.
2. **Including** posts/repos almost nobody is talking about yet if Jev says the impact is large.
3. **Not only** the quiet ones. A widely shipped Grok Build CLI bump, a lab model drop, and a 20-view primitive all belong if they would change what the user learns or builds.

Popularity is never a reason to drop. Recaps with no new artifact are.

## Ranking

- **All**: every kept signal, **newest first** (fastest update).
- **Hidden**: quiet + impact (the misses).
- **Impact**: highest Jev composite.
- **Practice**: changes how engineers use AI to do real work (TypeSafe is the *example of impact*, not the search target).

## Fetch

Pull both high-visibility shipping news and low-engagement substance. Do not keyword-lock on TypeSafe/Jev clones.
